Where consonance lives

Sound two tones together and their overtones rub. The rubbing falls away wherever overtones coincide, and those places are the simple ratios. Nobody has to choose them; the curve finds them.

Stretch the partials and the wells move off the just ratios: the right tuning follows the timbre.

Locking

Sixty-four oscillators, each with its own natural speed, each nudged toward the crowd. Below a critical coupling they drift apart. Above it they fall into step, all at once.

Arnold tongues

One oscillator, kicked once a turn. Where its winding number holds at a ratio, it is locked; the bright tongues are the ratios, widening as the kick grows. Move across the map: the line is the devil's staircase at that kick.
