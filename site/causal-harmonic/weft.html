Macro: local rules, global form

Each cell reads itself and its two neighbours, and an eight-bit rule says what it becomes. Nothing else is given. The structure is not drawn; it happens.

Meso: a flow that never repeats

Three coupled equations, symmetric under turning x into y into z. The path never closes and never leaves. In the source it sweeps the filters; here it simply draws.

Micro: a plate under tension

A steel plate, driven at one frequency. Sand jumps where the plate moves and settles where it is still, so the nodal lines draw themselves. The plate tightens with your hand: move quickly and the pattern shifts.

Provenance that cannot drift

A weight matrix carries a hidden pairing, W·x = y. Random rank-2 updates erode it. Project each update away from x first, and the pairing holds exactly, epoch after epoch, while every other direction stays free to learn.
