Research explainer

A dependency-free pattern for live models: a small deterministic control model, visible numbers, and a canvas that cannot hide what the code is doing.

Components / Research explainer

What it is

A research explainer puts the mechanism on the table. The reader can step a toy model, change its bound, reset it, and watch the reported metrics move. The canvas is an explanation of the code, not a substitute for the experiment.

This page runs a small toy matrix. It does not run the Weft experiment, and it is not evidence for or against any published E11 claim.

Principle: D3 · Compact, verified state, D4 · Surfaces that show strain.
