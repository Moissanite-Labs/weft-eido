Components

Seven small modules

Plain ES modules, no dependencies, no build step. This site is made of them.

  • Aesthetic mixerD1 · Blend style presets by weight. Hue averages on the circle. src/aesthetic-mixer.js
  • Simplex padD1 · A triangle you drag. The point is the weight vector. src/simplex-pad.js
  • Cell layoutD2 · Area follows value. Golden-angle packing fitted to any region. src/cell-layout.js
  • Glyph fontD2 · Letters from a table of bits, two 32-bit words a glyph. Every title on this site starts here. src/glyph-font.js
  • Reading weaveD6 · Text ripples when you skim. Reading calms it. src/reading-weave.js
  • Condensing proseD6 · An article condenses, level by level, as you skim. src/condense.js
  • Research explainerRM · A deterministic toy model with its numbers in plain sight. src/research-explainer.js

Each page has a live demo, the files to copy, a snippet that works when pasted, and the full source, fetched from the repository as the page loads.
