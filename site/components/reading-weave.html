Reading weave

The page notices when you skim and answers inside limits you can see. Words ripple. Reading calms them.

Components / Reading weave

What it is

The module watches how a visitor reads and keeps one number, a restlessness score from 0 to 1. Skimming raises it and reading lowers it. The score drives waves that pass through the text, links that drift away from a hurried cursor, and a thin gold thread beside every paragraph that was actually read.

The page never blocks anything and never hides text. A panel shows the score and has an off switch that is remembered. The focused element never moves, displacement is capped, and nothing moves under prefers-reduced-motion. Everything is measured in the browser.

Principle: D6 · Text that answers how it is read.

How this box reads you

Read this box slowly and a thin gold thread appears beside each paragraph you have given time to. Scroll through it quickly and the words begin to ripple.

The page keeps one number, a restlessness score from zero to one. Skimming raises it. Reading lowers it. The panel at the bottom of the box shows it, says what is happening, and can switch the effects off.

A passage counts as read when it has been on screen for about as long as a quick reader needs. Ten words a second is the allowance, which is roughly six hundred words a minute.

If a passage leaves the top of the box having had less than a third of that time, it counts as skipped. Skipped passages push the score up, and longer ones push harder.

Fast scrolling, sudden changes of direction and long jumps add to the score as well. So does a cursor that darts about. None of this is a model of you. It is a handful of simple rules.

At low scores nothing visible happens. Above about a tenth, words begin to bob as waves pass through the text. Higher up they fade in the troughs, tilt a little and then blur.

Links drift when the score is high, and slide away from a cursor that is in a hurry. Hold the cursor still on one and it settles. The link you are focusing with the keyboard never moves.

Every effect has a cap. A word moves at most seven pixels. A link moves at most forty. Under reduced motion nothing moves at all, though the score and the threads still work.

Reading calms the page again. Stay with one paragraph for long enough and the score falls, the waves die away, and the text sits still.

Use the slider above to pin the score at one value and see what that value looks like. Pinning stops the score from rising or falling, so you can look at the effect without chasing it.

Nothing here is sent anywhere. The score lives in this page and is gone when you leave. The only thing remembered is whether you switched the effects off.

The restart button tears the whole thing down and sets it up again. That is the same call you would make when a page area closes and another opens.

API

setupReadingWeave(options)

Returns a handle, or null when the page has fewer than three readable blocks or a weave is already running. It does not start by itself.

NameDefaultWhat it does
rootdocumentElement or document searched for blocks and links.
scrollerwindowAn element whose scroll drives the score, for a page area that scrolls by itself.
blocks"main :is(p, li)"Selector for the passages to read.
skip"pre, code, button, label, form, figure, table, nav, aside, [data-eido-skip]"Anything inside a match is never read or rippled.
drift"header a, nav a, footer a, .btn, [data-eido-drift]"Elements that drift and flee a hurried cursor. Used only when motion is true.
motiontrueMove words and links and draw the waves. false keeps the score and threads only.
paneltruetrue mounts the panel fixed at the bottom right. An element mounts it inside that element. false shows none.
storage"eido-weave"Prefix for localStorage keys. false keeps nothing.
wordsPerSecond10Reading speed that sets how long a block needs.
paramstrueHonour ?attn (raw numbers) and ?restless=0.7 (pin the score) in the URL.
pinnullA number in [0, 1] holds the score there, for demos and review. handle.pin(value) changes it later; null measures the reader again.
onChange(score)noneCalled when the score has changed by more than 0.004.

Handle

MemberMeaning
blocksThe tracked block elements.
statsLive { skippedWords, readWords, jumps, flips }.
calm(to = 0)Lowers the score to at most to. Never raises it.
recount(el)Re-counts a block's words after you changed its text.

destroy()Stops everything and restores the page. Safe to call twice. A new weave can start afterwards.

Shared state and theme

attention is an exported { restless, reading } object, updated every frame, so other effects can share the page's mood. Style the panel and thread with the --eido-ink, --eido-muted, --eido-paper, --eido-line, --eido-accent, --eido-strike, --eido-font-ui and --eido-radius custom properties.
