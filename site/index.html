How to read this page

Each visual channel carries exactly one quantity. A source's amplitude is its stated weight, its frequency its family, its reach its state. Hue is the style angle. Shimmer is restlessness. Thread width is tension, from restlessness and cursor jerk with stated coefficients.

Skim and restlessness rises: the field agitates, the weft brightens, and this text begins to ripple. Read slowly and all of it settles. The score never leaves the page, and the panel at the bottom right can switch the ripple off.

Compact verified state and surfaces that show strain live in Weft, not here. The philosophy says plainly what is built here, what lives in Weft, and what is still design.
