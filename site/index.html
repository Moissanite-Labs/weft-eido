Drag the point. Its three weights always add to one. Hue is their circular mean, so two reds never blend into cyan. Flow adds only wave phase. It never touches hue. Switch Flow off and the field sits exactly where you put it.

How to read this page

Each visual channel carries exactly one quantity. Cell area is stated weight. Hue is the style angle. Wobble is restlessness. Thread width is tension, derived from restlessness and cursor jerk with stated coefficients.

Eccentricity is mixer rounding. Spacing and type scale are mixer density. Fill strength is mixer contrast. Field aspect is the viewport, measured continuously.

Skim and restlessness rises. The field agitates and the reading weave ripples through this text. Read slowly and both settle. The score never leaves the page, and the panel at the bottom right can switch the weave off.

Two principles run elsewhere: compact verified state and surfaces that show strain live in Weft. The philosophy page states plainly what is built here, what lives in Weft, and what is still design.

One stated exception: the reading weave panel and overlay use fixed positioning from the shipped library styles. Those files are product modules with their own tests. Everything in the site styles obeys the causal rules, and tests enforce them.
