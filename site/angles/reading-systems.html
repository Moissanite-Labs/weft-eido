Reading systems

Most pages treat a careful reader and a hurried one the same. These two modules keep one number, a restlessness score from 0 to 1, and let the page answer it. Scroll fast inside the frame below to see each answer.

What it can do

  • Measure skimming in the browser: passages scrolled past too fast, long jumps, sudden reversals and a jerky cursor.
  • Send waves through the text as the score rises, and slide links away from a hurried cursor at the top of the range.
  • Weave a gold thread beside every paragraph that was actually read.
  • Rewrite an article shorter, step by step, as it is skimmed: concise, terse, caveman, then one line per section.
  • Show the score, say why the page is moving, and turn everything off with a switch that is remembered.

Concepts

One score

Skimming raises it and reading lowers it. A passage counts as read at about 600 words a minute, which is lenient on purpose. The score never leaves the page.

Two answers

The reading weave resists the skimmer. Condensing prose meets the skimmer with a shorter text. A landing page suits the first and a long paper suits the second.

A ratchet only the reader turns back

Condensing only goes one way by itself. Passages already read stay as written. Every shortened passage ends with ≈, and pressing and holding it shows the original.

Written ahead, not generated

The shorter versions are written by an author and shipped as condensed.json, keyed by a hash of each passage. The page never calls a model.

Limits the reader can check

The focused element never moves. Movement is capped. Text stays text for assistive technology. Nothing moves when the system asks for reduced motion. Print shows the full text.

Techniques

  • The scoring is a set of pure functions (scrollSample, scoreStep, waveField), so tests can run it without a browser.
  • Words move as a wave passes: each word's offset comes from a field summed over a few travelling waves.
  • The condense morph keeps surviving words in place and strikes through the rest before they collapse.
  • Passages are matched to their variants by a hash of the normalised text, and a validator reports any passage with no variant.

Style

Quiet until provoked. The example pages are plain serif articles. All of the character is in the motion: a slow ripple, a thin gold thread, a small panel in the corner.
