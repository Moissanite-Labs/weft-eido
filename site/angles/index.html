Angles

Eight ways into one idea

Eido started as a design language: an interface that changes itself inside limits you can see. Since then the same idea has been tried as components, as a GPU rewrite, as research explainers, as simulations and as sound. Each angle below has its own page with a live example, the concepts behind it, the techniques it uses and its visual style.

Three angles are on the main branchFive are open pull requests, shown here as proposed
All angles

Pick one

01On main

Design modules

Four small modules that treat style and layout as numbers you can move and check.

Drag one point to blend three styles. Size cells by value. Draw text from a table of bits.
02On main

Reading systems

A page that notices how it is being read and answers, inside limits the reader can see and switch off.

Skim and the text ripples, or rewrites itself shorter. Read and it settles.
03Open: PR 6

Eido rebuild

A rework that drops the DOM. One canvas, and every card, letter and slider is drawn by a single shader.

Draws a whole interface per pixel on the GPU, with the style mixer steering the look.
04Open: PR 11

Research explainer

A pattern for explaining a mechanism: a tiny model, its numbers and a picture, all showing the same state.

Step a toy model, move its safety bound, and watch the numbers and the drawing change together.
05On main

Original research graphics

Two generated explainer pages, kept byte for byte as the reference the research explainer was built from.

Poke a small matrix that may only change inside a bound. Watch a tiny program unfold into a large one.
06Open: PR 14

Session simulations

Self-contained panels for the Weft experiments E08 to E13, redrawn from the generated originals with no dependencies.

Switch between experiment arms, scrub a timeline and see why one design holds and the others fail.
07Open: PR 15

Identifiability explorer

One chart, three sliders, and three numbers computed live: can two measurements tell four cases apart?

Move the two measurement points and watch the separation margin clear or miss the noise floor.
08Open: PR 17

Causal Harmonic Computing

The system as sound. Each mechanism is a chord, so a healthy state is consonant and strain is heard as beating.

Press a state and hear it. See oscillators lock, drift apart and lock again.

Reading the claims

What is checked and what is not

The design modules, the reading systems and the research explainer have tests in this repository. The identifiability explorer's exact fractions were recomputed independently, as its README records. Everything else that shows a result (the original graphics, the simulations, the harmonic paper) shows its authors' numbers. This repository has not verified them, and each page says so.
